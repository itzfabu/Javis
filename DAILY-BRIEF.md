([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/stock-market-today-dow-s-p-live-updates))

([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/spain-declares-national-emergency-over-wildfires-around-madrid), [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/27/world/live-news/france-spain-wildfires-evacuations))

([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/23/google-1-billion-eu-fine-dma.html))

([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/investors-july-29-critical-day-130200956.html))
