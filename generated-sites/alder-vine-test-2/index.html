Est. in a 19th‑century townhouse

A townhouse, transformed

Alder & Vine sits behind an old brick facade, three rooms deep, each one a little lower-lit and a little quieter than the last.

We kept the original floors, the crown moulding, the fireplace that doesn't quite draw right — and built a wine list and a kitchen around them.

No tasting notes read off a card, no rush to turn the table. Just good pours and small plates, made for lingering.

Evenings only — the room is made for candlelight, not daylight.
