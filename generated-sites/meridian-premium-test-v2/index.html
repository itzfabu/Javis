Meridian is nineteen architects, three structural collaborators, and one shared conviction: the best massing study is the one that doesn't need to be redrawn after the zoning board sees it. We work directly with developers and their lenders from the first site walk, not after the entitlement is at risk.

Every unbuilt option we walk a client through is a form we're not attached to. That's the job. — Elena Marsh, Founding Principal

Founded in 2009, the studio has stayed deliberately small. We turn down more briefs than we accept, and we've never opened a second office.
