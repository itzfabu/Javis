### Recurring themes
- **"Real/dynamic beats templated/decorative" as the core differentiator.** Both live monetization projects bet on the same wedge: Website Generator competes against platform-locked template builders (Wix, Squarespace) by shipping real static code; Streamer HUD Widget competes against pre-rendered overlay loops (Etsy, OWN3D) with genuine Web-Audio-driven reactivity. Same competitive thesis applied to two different products.
- **AI/tech sophistication doesn't automatically earn a price premium.** Jarvis-as-a-Service was shelved after business-analyst research found "Claude Code setup" commoditized at $15-45 on Fiverr; Streamer HUD's own market research found the one direct "AI/reactive" comparable (Kudos.tv) sells at the same $12 tier as purely decorative widgets. Both pivots landed on the same lesson from opposite directions — the market pays for the concrete outcome (a widget, a lead-gen demo), not for "AI-powered" as a label.
- **Evidentiary-rigor discipline runs through both a project note and a resource note.** Jarvis System.md repeatedly separates "verified in code" from "plausible but unconfirmed" from "TASKS.md says X" for nearly every claim; generational-wealth-billionaires.md does the same with its "Primary-source status by claim" section distinguishing traced-to-primary-source stats from industry-repeated claims. Worth treating as a house style worth keeping consistent as more notes get added.
